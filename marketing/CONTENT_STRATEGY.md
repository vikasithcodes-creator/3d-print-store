## 📈 Posting & Optimization Playbook for Instagram & YouTube

### 1. The 3-Second Retention Rule
* **Never start with a logo or introduction.** Start directly inside the action (filament extruding, bed flexing, or immediate visual movement).
* Keep cuts under 2.5 seconds during the first 6 seconds.

### 2. Audio Strategy
* **Instagram/TikTok:** Pair the video with trending audio (remixed lo-fi hip-hop or upbeat house), but keep the original SFX (bed pop, nozzle hum) at 40% volume layered underneath for ASMR gratification.
* **YouTube Shorts:** Use original crisp sound design with high punch.

### 3. Comment Section Farming (Algorithm Hacks)
* In the caption, ask an open-ended subjective question:
  - *"What should we print next? Top comment gets printed."*
  - *"Rate this finish from 1 to 10."*
  - *"What colorway would you rock on your keys?"*

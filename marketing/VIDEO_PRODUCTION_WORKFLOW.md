## 🎬 Production Overview

This document contains everything you need to produce 4 high-retention short-form videos for SM Studio using free AI video generation tools.

**Total Production Time:** 2-3 hours
**Tools Required:** Free accounts on Haiper AI, Runway ML, Luma AI, Pika Labs + CapCut (free editor)
**Output Format:** 9:16 vertical (1080 × 1920), optimized for Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts

### Audio & Sound Design

**Background Music:**
- **Source:** Uppbeat.io (free, no attribution) or Pixabay
- **Style:** Lo-fi hip-hop beat with heavy 808 bass and airy synth chords
- **BPM:** 85-95 BPM (slow, satisfying pace)
- **Keywords to search:** "lofi chill beat", "trap minimal instrumental"

**Sound Effects (Layer over music at 40% volume):**
- 0:02 - Mechanical printer hum (low hum)
- 0:04 - Crisp magnetic bed SNAP/POP (loud, punchy)
- 0:07 - Subtle articulated joint clicking (ASMR)
- 0:10 - Soft whoosh transition
- **Source:** Pixabay Sound Effects (https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/)

### Text Overlays (CapCut)

**Font:** Bold Sans-Serif (Impact, Montserrat Black, or CapCut "Bebas Neue")
**Color:** White text with 60% black shadow/outline
**Position:** Center or lower-third
**Animation:** Fade in + slight scale (0.3s)

| Timecode | Text |
|----------|------|
| 0:00 - 0:02 | "Zero assembly. Printed in one piece." |
| 0:02 - 0:06 | "10 hours of print time in 10 seconds." |

---

### CapCut Editing Steps

1. **Create New Project** → Select 9:16 format (1080 × 1920)
2. **Import all 6 generated video clips**
3. **Timeline Assembly:**
   - Drag Scene 1 → 0:00
   - Scene 2 → 0:02
   - Scene 3 → 0:04
   - Scene 4 → 0:06
   - Scene 5 → 0:10
   - Scene 6 → 0:12
4. **Add Transitions:**
   - Between Scene 3-4: "Zoom In" (0.2s)
   - Between Scene 5-6: "Whip Pan" (0.3s)
5. **Color Grading:**
   - Apply CapCut preset: "Moody" or "Cinematic Warm"
   - Increase Contrast: +10%
   - Increase Clarity: +15%
6. **Add Music Track** (trim to 14 seconds, fade out last 0.5s)
7. **Add SFX** (import individual sounds, place at exact timestamps)
8. **Add Text** (use timestamps above, white bold font, black stroke)
9. **Export Settings:**
   - Resolution: 1080 × 1920
   - Frame Rate: 30 FPS
   - Bitrate: Smart HDR (highest quality)
   - Format: MP4

## ⚡ Pro Tips for Maximum Retention

### The 1-Second Rule
- First frame MUST be visually striking (macro shot, motion, color contrast)
- Never start with a logo or intro

### The 3-Cut Rule
- First 6 seconds should have at least 3 cuts to maintain pace
- Each cut on the beat of the music

### The Loop Trick (Videos 1 & 4)
- Last frame should visually match first frame
- Enables infinite replay loop = higher watch time %

### Comment Farming
- End captions with a question to boost comments
- Example: "What should we print next? Top comment wins."
